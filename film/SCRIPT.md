# Narration script

For recording your own voice, or for speaking live over `SPHEREx-Explorer-film-no-voice.mp4`.
Each line starts at its time on the film; the times are where the synthetic take sits, so a
spoken line should start there and end before the next one. The ids name the files for recorded
takes (`voice/<id>.wav`).

Read it slowly, with weight, like the opening of a documentary. Pauses matter more than speed:
the picture carries most of the story.
